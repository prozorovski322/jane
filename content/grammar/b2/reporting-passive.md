troubles: word-order

## Два способа

| It is … that                              | Кто-то is … to                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| It is said that the company is the best.  | The company is said to be the best.     |
| It is believed that goldfish forget fast. | Goldfish are believed to forget fast.   |
| It is thought that it started long ago.   | It is thought to have started long ago. |

> It is said that the wall can be seen from space. | Говорят, стену видно из космоса.
> Their work is said to be the best in the city. | Говорят, они лучше всех в городе.
> The myth is thought to have started a hundred years ago. | Считается, что миф появился сто лет назад.

## Сейчас и раньше

- He is said to live in London — говорят, он живёт в Лондоне (сейчас).
- He is said to have lived in London — говорят, он жил в Лондоне (раньше).

TIP: Так же работают know, expect, report, claim: Prices are expected to rise. — Ожидается, что цены вырастут.

## Типичные ошибки

! It is said the wall to be visible. => The wall is said to be visible. | Либо It is said that…, либо The wall is said to…
! He is said that he is rich. => He is said to be rich. | После he is said — to + глагол.
! The myth is thought to start long ago. => The myth is thought to have started long ago. | О прошлом — to have + V3.

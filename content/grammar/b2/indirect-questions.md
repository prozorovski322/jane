## Прямой и косвенный вопрос

| Прямой                 | Косвенный                                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| When is the interview? | Could you tell me when the interview is? |
| Where do you work?     | Could I ask where you work?              |
| When will you decide?  | Do you know when you will decide?        |
| Do you offer training? | I was wondering if you offer training.   |

> Could you tell me when you'll make a decision? | Не подскажете, когда вы примете решение?
> I was wondering if you offer flexible working hours. | Хотел бы уточнить, есть ли у вас гибкий график.
> Do you know whether the meeting has started? | Вы не знаете, совещание уже началось?
> Would you mind sending me the report? | Не могли бы вы прислать мне отчёт?

TIP: После Would you mind — форма на -ing: Would you mind waiting? Отвечают наоборот: No, not at all — значит «пожалуйста, мне не трудно».

## Типичные ошибки

! Could you tell me when is the interview? => Could you tell me when the interview is? | Прямой порядок слов.
! Do you know where does she work? => Do you know where she works? | Без does.
! I was wondering do you offer training. => I was wondering if you offer training. | Нужно if или whether.
! Would you mind to send me the report? => Would you mind sending me the report? | После mind — -ing.
